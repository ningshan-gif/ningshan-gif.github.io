Ive become moon watcher, moon watcher and Earth roamer
the moon is one ground truth
it’s the same moon when seen from the Bay Area or Asia or anywhere on earth. when I look up I feel connected to all beings and non-beings at once. temporally, people in the past of all civilizations stared at the moon (especially when they didn’t have screens to look at), the wonder in their gaze are reflected by the moon back into my eyes, it’s the same wonder across time: living beings wondering about being, how wonderful

What defines the fundamental living experience, I think it is not to output useful tokens but simply to feel and to be aware of feeling. 

today I leave the bay area again after two months, I will be back but before then, 但愿人长久，千里共婵娟
