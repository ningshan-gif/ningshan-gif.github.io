七夕祭 2025 in SFC, my first ever festival in Japan. Be it 運命/結び/縁, I felt deep human connection that day that is more and more rare in this age of superficiality. I felt so sincerely content and lucky to have met those around me. I felt Youth, not as a quality of age, but a state of mind. When the final hanabi bloomed to my favorite song, I thought and was sure that, we’ll continue to live life like a never-ending summer.
